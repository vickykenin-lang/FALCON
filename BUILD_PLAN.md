# FALCON Product Build Plan

## Part 1 — Foundation & Contracts — COMPLETE
Identity, isolated module boundaries, typed V1 events/missions and versioned contracts.

## Part 2 — Intelligence & Internal Nervous System — COMPLETE
Brain orchestration boundary, event bus and durable memory implementation.

## Part 3 — Autonomous Runtime & Action Foundation — COMPLETE
Heartbeat/runtime, mission intake/checkpointing, governance gate, execution adapter registry, CLI and foundation tests.

## Part 4 — Live Product & Real-World Capability — PENDING
Live Founder chat/dashboard, streaming activity, production model adapter, GitHub/browser/MCP adapters, durable scheduler deployment, authentication, observability, end-to-end acquisition stress test and production hardening.

Parts marked COMPLETE mean repository implementation is present. Production readiness is not claimed until Part 4 integration and live verification pass.
